About Us

GR & Sons Trout Fish Farm is located in the scenic village of Jura, near Ashkot in the heart of District Neelum, Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Situated alongside natural river water flows surrounded by lush green mountains, our farm utilizes pure alpine water ideal for raising premium quality trout.

Our Farm

Our facility features structured concrete raceway tanks designed to supply continuous fresh, highly oxygenated natural water directly from the mountains. This clean, flowing water environment ensures healthy growth conditions for our stock year-round.
